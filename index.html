Grasshopper

Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

The short version

  • Your purchases, savings, goals and settings are stored only in your browser, on your computer.
  • Grasshopper has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads and no analytics.
  • Something is sent only when you choose to send feedback or fill in the Grasshopper Plus interest form.
  • We never sell your data.

What Grasshopper does

Grasshopper is a Chrome extension that adds a Pause button next to checkout buttons on online stores. It shows what a purchase could grow to if you saved or invested the money instead, and keeps a record of the purchases you choose to skip.

Information stored on your computer

To do its job, Grasshopper keeps the following in Chrome’s extension storage on your device:

  • Purchases you skip or buy anyway: the item name, price, store website, date, category, and any shopping trigger you choose to log.
  • Your settings: age, retirement age, expected return rates, optional pay rate, goals, comparison items, no-spend hours, website rules and appearance.
  • Reminders you set, so Chrome can show them at the time you chose.
  • A random install ID, described below.

This information never leaves your computer. We can’t see it, and it isn’t synced to other devices.

If you turn on the optional passcode, this information is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key made from your passcode. The passcode itself isn’t stored, and it can’t be recovered if you forget it. A few settings stay readable while Grasshopper is locked, so your checkout rules keep working: sites where the Pause button is off, no-spend hours, website rules, appearance, and reminder times.

How Grasshopper reads web pages

Grasshopper runs on the web pages you visit so it can find checkout and “Buy now” buttons, and read the item names and prices near them. This happens entirely inside your browser. Grasshopper doesn’t record which pages you visit, doesn’t keep your browsing history, and doesn’t send page content anywhere. Nothing is saved unless you use the Pause panel.

You can turn Grasshopper off for any website from the toolbar popup.

Information you choose to send

Grasshopper sends information only when you fill in one of these forms and press send:

  • Send feedback, in Settings: your message and, if you add one, an email address for a reply.
  • Grasshopper Plus interest form: the features you’d use, whether you’d pay for them, any comment, and, if you add one, an email address so we can tell you when Plus is ready.

Each form also includes Grasshopper’s version number, your browser’s user agent (browser name and version), and the random install ID. The install ID is created on your computer and only lets us tell repeat responses apart. It isn’t linked to your name, your purchases or your browsing.

Your purchase history and savings are never included.

These forms are delivered through Formspree, a form service that passes responses on to us. Like any website, Formspree receives the network address your request comes from, and it handles submissions under its own privacy policy. We use what you send only to read your feedback, reply if you asked us to, gauge interest in Grasshopper Plus, and let you know when Plus is available. You can ask us to delete anything you’ve sent.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell, rent or trade your information.
  • We don’t use it for advertising, and we don’t share it with data brokers.
  • We don’t use it to decide creditworthiness or for lending.
  • We don’t run analytics or tracking scripts.

Grasshopper’s use of information follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Controlling and deleting your data

  • Export your history as a CSV file from Settings.
  • Delete individual entries from your history.
  • Erase everything with Delete all data in Settings.
  • Uninstalling Grasshopper removes all of its stored data from Chrome.

Future paid features

Grasshopper Plus isn’t available yet. If paid features are added, payments will be handled by a payment provider, and we’ll update this policy before then to explain what that involves.

Children

Grasshopper isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects what leaves your computer, we’ll also mention it in Grasshopper’s release notes.

Contact

For questions about this policy or to ask us to delete something you sent, use Send feedback at the bottom of Grasshopper’s Settings.

Grasshopper’s projections are illustrations based on a steady yearly return. They aren’t financial advice.
